Mississaugashowroom

The Greater Toronto showroom, near Pearson. Large-format slabs, SPC boards and a contractor-friendly counter, open seven days.

Get directions Book a consultation

Mississauga serves Toronto and the GTA from Maingate Drive, a short drive from the 401 and 403. It is the only Club Ceramic showroom open on Sundays, which makes it popular for weekend renovation planning.

Questions

Are you open on Sundays?

Yes. Mississauga is open Sunday 10 am to 3 pm, as well as Monday to Friday 8:30 am to 5 pm and Saturday 10 am to 3 pm.

Which number do I call from Toronto?

Call +1 647 527 6030. The Mississauga team handles Toronto and GTA enquiries.

Can contractors pick up larger orders?

Yes. Speak with the team ahead of time so the order is staged and ready when you arrive.

Is the Montreal flyer pricing available here?

The monthly flyer is valid at Montreal. Ask the Mississauga team for current pricing on the same lines.
