Installing large formats: adhesive, levelling and grout

What goes under a 24×48 matters as much as the tile. A plain-language guide for homeowners and installers.

Installation, 6 min read, Club Ceramic

Large tiles are less forgiving than small ones. A flat substrate, the right adhesive and a levelling system make the difference between a floor that looks like a slab and one with lipped edges.

Flatness first

Check the floor with a long straightedge. Large formats need a flatter substrate than small tile; fill dips and grind high spots before you start.

Adhesive and coverage

Use a large-format, polymer-modified thinset and back-butter each tile. Aim for full coverage, especially in showers and on floors, and follow the manufacturer’s trowel size.

Levelling systems

Clips and wedges hold neighbouring edges flush while the adhesive cures. On 24×48 and 32×64 they are close to essential.

Joints and grout

Keep joints consistent, match grout colour to the tile for a seamless look, and include movement joints at perimeters and changes of plane.
