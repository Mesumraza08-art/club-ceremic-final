Questions

Can I visit on a Sunday?

Yes, by appointment. Book a consultation online and choose Ottawa, or call +1 613 601 1474.

What are the weekday hours?

Monday to Friday 9 am to 5 pm, and Saturday 10 am to 3 pm.

Do you serve Gatineau?

Yes. Customers from across the National Capital Region visit the Nepean showroom.

Can I plan a whole bathroom here?

That is what the showroom is set up for. Bring measurements and photos and the team will build a board with you.
