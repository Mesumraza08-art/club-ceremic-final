AboutClub Ceramic

A manufacturer and distributor of ceramic and porcelain tile, serving European and North American markets for more than ten years.

Our approach

The best quality of tiles at the best prices, to help you build the places you have in mind.

Our designers develop colour and pattern for a changing market, our facilities work to international standards, and our showrooms in Quebec and Ontario put the whole range in front of homeowners and trade customers alike.
