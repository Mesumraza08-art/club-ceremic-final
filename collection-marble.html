TheMarble Edit

Statuario, calacatta, onyx and crema in porcelain. The movement of real marble, with none of the sealing.

Collection 01

Timeless stone character. Engineered for modern spaces.

Marble is chosen for the veining. Porcelain lets you choose the vein, the scale and the shine, then lay it in a shower without worrying about etching.
