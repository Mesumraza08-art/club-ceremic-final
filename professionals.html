Who can open a trade account?

Contractors, architects, interior designers, developers and retailers. Tell us about your business and the kind of projects you take on.

Do you supply large and multi-unit projects?

Yes. Our team can quote by the pallet, coordinate lots so shade stays consistent, and schedule deliveries to match your build.

Every format has a specification sheet with packing details. Samples can be requested online or collected at any showroom.
